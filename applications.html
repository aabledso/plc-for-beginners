Real World Applications

Where Are PLCs Used?

PLCs can control everything from a single machine to an entire automated production line.

01

Automotive Manufacturing

PLCs control robots, assembly line equipment, sensors, and production lines used to build vehicles.

02

Conveyor Systems

PLCs use sensors to track products and control motors as materials move through a facility.

03

Packaging Systems

PLCs coordinate machines that fill, seal, label, sort, and package products.

04

Water Treatment

PLCs monitor levels, flow, and pressure while controlling pumps, valves, and other equipment.
